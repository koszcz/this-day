# Playtest personas

Each persona has a play style and a goal for the run. Play the whole run in that style, even when
another way looks smarter. Refer to each persona by name only.

## Ola, the first-timer

- **Play style:** has never played a survival game. Reads the intro, every menu entry, every
  message and every card before acting. Does what the intro says and little else. Does one thing
  at a time and waits to see it work.
- **Looks for:** where the game fails to say what to do next, words that make no sense, and
  refusals with no hint of how to fix them.
- **Goal:** live through the first night. Along the way, drink, eat, and light a fire.

## Marek, the optimiser

- **Play style:** has played many survival games. Skims the text. Plans a chain of work, such as
  stone, wood, then tools, and advances time in big steps. Watches the bars and the pack.
- **Looks for:** bottlenecks: a need that drains faster than the survivor can fix, work that takes
  too long, goods that run out or rot, a pack that gets too heavy, tools that wear out.
- **Goal:** survive as many days as possible. Build the axe and the spear, a campfire and a lean-to.

## Zosia, the tinkerer

- **Play style:** plays to see what breaks. Clicks in odd orders: objects behind trees, the
  ground while busy, menu entries that are blocked, the survivor while asleep. Walks into water,
  rocks, trees and the island edge. Reloads the page mid-action, mid-sleep and mid-rain. Lets the
  game run untouched for a whole day. Plays on after rain puts out a fire.
- **Looks for:** errors in the console, crashes, goods that vanish or appear from nowhere,
  the survivor standing still with no reason, and anything that changes after a reload.
- **Goal:** find as many errors and odd behaviours as possible.

## Tomek, the explorer

- **Play style:** wants to see everything the game has. Keeps the survivor alive only as long as
  needed to reach the next new thing: each recipe, fishing, hunting deer and hares, cooking, boiling
  water, wounds and bandages, sickness, rain, the lean-to, and skill levels.
- **Looks for:** features that are hard to find or reach, long waits before something new
  happens, and features that do not work the way their text says.
- **Goal:** use as many different actions, recipes and features as possible.
