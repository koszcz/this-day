- **Jointed survivor**: the survivor gets a head, torso, two arms and two legs, built from low-poly shapes in code. The legs and arms swing while walking, and the figure breathes slightly when idle. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Roaming animals**: low-poly deer and hares wander the island and flee when you come near. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Infection**: a wound left untreated for 3 game hours turns into infection and sickness, and a clean bandage clears it. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Crafting steps**: the axe and spear need a stone head, a handle and rope, made one step at a time. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Wounds**: slips on rock and spear fishing can cause bleeding that drains health, or a sprain that slows your walk. Rest or a plant-fibre bandage treats them. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Food spoils**: raw fish and berries rot after 6 game hours in your pack, and cooked fish lasts 24. Eating rotten food makes you sick. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Plant fibre**: bushes give plant fibre, and a new Cordage recipe makes it into rope. *Fun 1 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Fire-making**: a campfire needs tinder and kindling, and lighting it can fail. Rain puts out a fire with no lean-to beside it. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Carry weight**: the pack has a weight limit, and a heavy load slows your walk and drains energy faster. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Tool wear**: the axe breaks after 20 uses and the spear after 12, and you craft a new one. The pack shows uses left. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Dirty water**: drinking sea or pond water makes you sick 40% of the time. A new "Boil and drink" action at a lit campfire makes it safe. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Rain and wet**: random rain spells darken the sky. Outside a lean-to you get wet, and wet pulls warmth down fast until you dry by a lit fire or in the lean-to. No wind and no seasons. *Fun 3 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
