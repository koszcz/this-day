## Later

Triaged and ready to build, but not next.
- **Food spoils**: raw fish and berries rot after 6 game hours in your pack, and cooked fish lasts 24. Eating rotten food makes you sick. *Fun 2 · Effort M*
- **Wounds**: slips on rock and spear fishing can cause bleeding that drains health, or a sprain that slows your walk. Rest or a plant-fibre bandage treats them. *Fun 2 · Effort M · Needs: Plant fibre*
- **Infection**: a wound left untreated for 3 game hours turns into infection and sickness, and a clean bandage clears it. *Fun 2 · Effort S · Needs: Wounds*
- **Crafting steps**: the axe and spear need a stone head, a handle and cordage, made one step at a time. *Fun 2 · Effort M · Needs: Plant fibre*
- **Roaming animals**: low-poly deer and hares wander the island and flee when you come near. *Fun 2 · Effort M*
- **Hunt with a spear**: speared animals drop meat, which is a new food that must be cooked at a fire. *Fun 2 · Effort S · Needs: Roaming animals*
- **Jointed survivor**: the survivor gets a head, torso, two arms and two legs, built from low-poly shapes in code. The legs and arms swing while walking, and the figure breathes slightly when idle. *Fun 2 · Effort M*
- **Action animations**: each action has its own animation, driven by the current busy action: chopping, swinging a spear to fish, bending to pick berries, crouching to drink, tending a fire to cook, and eating. *Fun 2 · Effort M · Needs: Jointed survivor*
- **Held tools**: the axe and spear appear in the survivor's hand when used or carried, and the survivor lies flat with arms and legs out to sleep. *Fun 1 · Effort S · Needs: Jointed survivor*

## Done

Newest first, with the date it was merged.

- **Plant fibre**: bushes give plant fibre, and a new Cordage recipe makes it into rope. *Fun 1 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Fire-making**: a campfire needs tinder and kindling, and lighting it can fail. Rain puts out a fire with no lean-to beside it. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Carry weight**: the pack has a weight limit, and a heavy load slows your walk and drains energy faster. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Tool wear**: the axe breaks after 20 uses and the spear after 12, and you craft a new one. The pack shows uses left. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Dirty water**: drinking sea or pond water makes you sick 40% of the time. A new "Boil and drink" action at a lit campfire makes it safe. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Rain and wet**: random rain spells darken the sky. Outside a lean-to you get wet, and wet pulls warmth down fast until you dry by a lit fire or in the lean-to. No wind and no seasons. *Fun 3 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04

- **Mouse and touch only**: WASD, the arrow keys, the number keys, `E` and Escape no longer do anything. You walk only by clicking or tapping the ground, and the help line shows no keys. *Fun 2 · Effort S* — 2026-11-04
- **Click an object for its actions**: clicking an object opens a small HUD beside it with that object's actions, such as Chop tree, Pick berries, Drink, Fish, Cook fish or Add wood to the fire. Choosing an action walks you to the object first, then does it. Clicking yourself opens Sleep, Eat and Craft, and the bottom action bar goes. *Fun 3 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
- **Hover highlights**: trees, bushes, stones, water, fires, lean-tos and your character glow and show a pointer cursor when the mouse is over them. On touch a tap shows the same glow as the HUD opens. *Fun 2 · Effort M* — 2026-11-04
