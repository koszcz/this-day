# Playtest: Ola, the first-timer on seed 35732465

- Commit: eaea98a Add held tools
- Played: 9 minutes of the 20 allowed, to game time 00:28 on day 2 (about 1,050 game minutes). The tester stopped early because the survivor was sick, soaked, at 0% water and 0% warmth, with no way left to fix it in time.
- Goal for this run: finish as many goals from the goal panel as possible. This build has no goal panel (finding 1). The tester used the start text instead: "Find water. Find food. Keep warm when the sun goes down."

## What the tester achieved

- Gathered 3 stone, 2 wood and 2 berries. Made 1 stone head (day 1, 23:31). Labor reached level 2 (14:42).
- Ate rotten berries on day 2, 00:28, and got sick.
- Did not find water. Did not eat fresh food. Did not make a fire or a shelter. None of the three start-text goals was met.
- End state: health 36%, food 30%, water 0%, energy 20%, warmth 2%. Raining, soaked, sick. Pack: 2 wood, 1 stone, 1 stone head, 1 rotten berry.
