# Playtest: Marek, the optimiser on island 2152029426

- Commit: eaea98a Add held tools
- Played: about 14 minutes of wall time, to game day 2 00:40 (first life), then about 6 game hours of a second life
- Goal for this run: reach the highest population possible. The game has one survivor and no population, so the goal became: survive as long as possible and chain crafts fast (see finding 1).

## What the tester achieved

First life, in order (game time):

1. 08:07 Gathered 2 stone (cut myself, bleeding).
2. 10:47 Made a bandage from 2 fibre and applied it from the survivor's own menu.
3. 11:08 Made a stone head.
4. 16:23 Made a handle.
5. 18:21 Made cordage and a stone axe.
6. 20:44 Made a campfire.
7. 21:27 Gathered kindling, then tinder (21:59).
8. 22:26 Tried to light the fire in the rain. It failed and used up the tinder and kindling.
9. Day 2 00:40 Died of thirst. The game said "You lasted 2 days."

No water source was found. Thirst, warmth and energy were all at zero by 20:00. Never built a lean-to or a spear. Never ate beyond 1 berry.

Second life (after "Wake up again", day 1 07:00 again, skills reset to level 1): walked for about 6 game hours looking for water. None found. Ended at 12:58, health full, thirst about 35%.

## How to see each finding again

All use seed 2152029426 and `http://localhost:5174/?seed=2152029426`. The game cannot be paused or sped up, so times are approximate.

1. Open the page. `window.playtest` is `undefined`.
2. Click "Open your eyes". Click a rock and choose "Gather stone". Repeat once or twice until "You cut yourself" appears (findings 3).
3. Strip fibre twice at a bush, craft a Bandage, click the survivor, choose "Bandage wound" (finding 3).
4. Strip fibre on a bush. Stand next to the bush. Click a tree behind or beside it. Choose "Break branches" or "Chop tree". Nothing happens (findings 5 and 7). A click far away on open ground frees him.
5. Craft Campfire (3 wood, 2 stone). Gather kindling at a tree and tinder at a bush. Wait for rain (around 21:30 on day 1 here). Click the campfire, "Light fire". Both items vanish (finding 8).
6. Do not drink. Watch thirst reach zero around 20:00-day 2 00:40 and the death screen (findings 10 and 12).
7. Walk through a dense stand of trees at night to see the survivor vanish (finding 11).
