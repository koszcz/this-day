# Playtest: Zosia on island (random; the ?seed= link does nothing)

- Commit: eaea98a Add held tools
- Played: about 9 minutes of wall time, to game time 17 h (day 2, 00:07) on the first run, death by thirst. A second run started after "Wake up again" and was not played.
- Goal for this run: find as many errors and odd behaviours as possible

## What the tester achieved

- Opened the game, cut kindling and wood, picked berries (2), found 1 stone.
- Slept once in the rain (day 1 12:45 to 16:00). Ate a rotten berry and got sick.
- Idled to see what happens. Died of thirst on day 2 at 00:07. No water, fire or shelter was built.
- Pressed "Wake up again": a new game started at day 1, 07:00. No console errors.
- Not covered: language switch (the page has none), building near water, save then load of my own game (the shared save made it impossible).

## How to see each finding again

Seeds are random, so these are click paths from a new game (steps from "Open your eyes").
1. (1, 13) Open `http://localhost:5174/?seed=1`, run `typeof window.playtest` (gives "undefined"). Compare the island with `?seed=2`. Same logic, random island.
2. (2) Click a berry bush in dense forest, choose Pick berries, wait 30 s. Pack stays empty, no message. Compare with Gather kindling on a tree.
3. (3) Walk among pines. The survivor is hidden.
4. (4) Click the Cordage card with an empty pack. Nothing happens.
5. (5) Click a tree, Break branches, then click the ground. Nothing happens. Click the survivor, Sleep, then click the ground. Nothing happens until about 4 game hours pass.
6. (6, 7, 8) Click the survivor in the rain, choose Sleep. Wait for berries to rot (about 4 h), click the survivor, Eat rotten berries.
7. (9) Click the survivor at the default zoom. The menu runs past the bottom of a 1024x768 window.
8. (10) Open the game in two tabs on the same origin. Play in one, reload the other. Both read and write `this-day.save.v1` every 10 s.
9. (11, 12, 14) Do nothing. At about day 2, 00:07 the death card shows "You lasted 2 days".
